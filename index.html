        Questions

        What products does Nova Nutrition make?

        Nova Nutrition makes three supplements for lifters: Whey Protein for recovery, Mass Gainer for extra calories and size, and Creatine Monohydrate for strength. Every ingredient and amount is printed on the tub, with no proprietary blends.

        How much protein is in Nova Whey Protein?

        One 30 g scoop of Nova Whey Protein (Double Chocolate) has 25 g of protein, 5.5 g of naturally occurring BCAAs, 120 kcal, 3 g of carbohydrates, 1.5 g of sugar and 1.8 g of fat. It comes in 1 kg (33 servings), 2.27 kg (75 servings) and 4.5 kg (150 servings) tubs.

        Who is Nova Mass Gainer for?

        Nova Mass Gainer is for hard gainers who struggle to eat enough to grow. One 250 g serving (5 scoops) gives 1000 kcal, 50 g of protein from a whey and casein blend, 180 g of carbohydrates and 3 g of creatine monohydrate. It comes in 3 kg, 5 kg and 7 kg tubs.

        How do you take Nova Creatine?

        Take one 5 g scoop a day, mixed into water, juice or a protein shake. It is unflavoured, micronised creatine monohydrate with nothing added. A 300 g tub gives 60 servings, about two months at one scoop a day. It also comes in 500 g and 1 kg tubs.
