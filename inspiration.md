## Ideas that would beat XGENS and IRON FUEL

1. **A 3D product hero.** The tub or pouch turns, opens and splits apart as you scroll (the More Nutrition canvas technique). Neither old site does real 3D.
2. **A colour per flavour or product.** The whole page re-tints when you switch product (SPYLT and Ghost).
3. **A cinematic loader and page transitions** (ProSupps and Ciao).
4. **A "NOVA" space/star theme:** a starfield or light-burst motif, a premium dark palette with one glowing accent. This sets it apart from the lime (XGENS) and yellow (IRON FUEL) looks.
5. **Smooth scrolling** (Lenis) and **GSAP ScrollTrigger** for the story sections.
6. **Trust sections done well:** lab-test badges, a nutrition panel you can open up, ingredient sourcing.
7. **Mobile first.** The award sites build a separate mobile version of the 3D effect.
